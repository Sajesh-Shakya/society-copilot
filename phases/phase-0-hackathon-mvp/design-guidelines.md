# **design-guidelines.md**

## Emotional tone

Feels like a calm, competent assistant sitting beside you—quietly handling complexity so you don’t feel overwhelmed.

---

## Typography

* H1: 28px / semibold

* H2: 22px / medium

* Body: 16px / regular

* Caption: 14px

* Font: Inter (clean, neutral, readable)

* Line height: 1.5+

---

## Color system

* Primary: #2563EB (trust, clarity)
* Secondary: #F8FAFC (calm background)
* Accent: #10B981 (success)
* Warning: #F59E0B
* Error: #EF4444

---

## Spacing & layout

* 8pt grid
* large whitespace
* single-column focus

---

## Motion

* subtle fade (200ms)
* no aggressive animations
* success = gentle confirmation

---

## Voice & tone

* calm
* helpful
* non-judgmental

Examples:

* “You’re missing one thing: VAT receipt”
* “Looks good — ready for approval”

---

## System consistency

Inspired by:

* Linear (clarity)
* Notion (flexibility)
* Apple (calm UX)

---

## Accessibility

* AA contrast minimum
* keyboard navigation
* clear focus states

---

## Emotional audit

* Does this reduce stress?
* Does this remove thinking?
* Does this guide clearly?
